# Search Keywords for Agentic AI Jobs in Pakistan

**Date:** April 30, 2026

## Executive Summary

The agentic AI job market in Pakistan is experiencing significant growth, with hundreds of open positions available across multiple job boards. Effective job searching requires using specific keywords that align with industry terminology, ranging from broad "AI Agent" searches to specialized terms like "Agentic AI Developer" and "AI Agent Specialist." Key job platforms including Indeed, Glassdoor, Rozee.pk, and Bayt.com actively list these positions, primarily concentrated in major tech hubs like Lahore.

The most effective keywords for finding agentic AI jobs in Pakistan include:

Different platforms in Pakistan host varying volumes of agentic AI positions, making keyword selection platform-dependent:

## 3. Geographic and Specialized Considerations

Job seekers in Pakistan should consider geographic and specialization-based keywords to refine their searches:

### Location-Based Keywords
**Lahore** emerges as the primary hub for agentic AI employment, with multiple positions listed including roles at SourceTek in Johar Town and I.T. Path [1][4]. Including "Lahore," "Punjab," or "Remote" in searches narrows results to specific locations or distributed positions.

### Specialized Function Keywords
Beyond generic agentic AI terminology, specialized applications appear in the job market:
- **"Chatbots/Agentic AI"** — Indicates roles focusing on conversational AI systems [4]
- **"Python Developer (AI / LLM / Agentic Systems)"** — Targets language-model-focused positions [3]
- **"AI Developer Intern"** — Entry-level positions for skill development [3]
- **"AI Automation Developer"** — Focuses on automating workflows using AI agents [8]

Domain-specific applications mentioned in Pakistani developer communities include LinkedIn scraping agents, outreach agents, voice calling agents, and trend-detection agents [6].
